ClimaBuild Studios

Designing Affordability. Building for Climate.

A student-led design and research studio focused on affordable, environmentally responsive housing solutions in Nigeria.

Who We Are

ClimaBuild Studios operates at the intersection of climate science, architecture, and affordability. The motivation behind the business is rooted in the belief that affordable housing should not compromise environmental comfort, cultural relevance, or dignity, and that climate-responsive design is not a luxury, but a necessity in Nigeria’s changing climate.

Our Core Services

Climate-Responsive Housing Design

Passive design strategies that reduce heat gain and energy demand while improving indoor comfort.

Sustainable Building Materials

Evaluation of local materials and construction systems for cost-effective, sustainable housing delivery.

Low-Cost Residential Development

Design and delivery of affordable single-family, multi-unit, and incremental housing using efficient space planning and cost-effective construction methods.
